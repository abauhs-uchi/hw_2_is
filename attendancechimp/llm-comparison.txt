The student created models.py implementation is simpler and more sparse. I only 
implemented attributes directly requested by the instructions/design doc, while the 
AI version implements many possibly unneeded fields. For example, the AI StudentProfile
implements major fields even though we have not established that this system is for 
university students. It also implements alternate/related names for many  attributes, as 
well as __str__ methods for the classes.


For the QRcode upload, the AI model implements several anti-cheating measure, while 
my only anti-cheating measure is tracking upload time to ensure students are uploading
during the correct times.
